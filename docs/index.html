🌧 Rainy Desk

Ambient rain & lightning app for iOS

Frequently Asked Questions

Why doesn't the lightning flash work when my screen is off?
iOS only allows camera flash access while the app is in the foreground. The rain sound continues in the background, but the flash effect requires the screen to be on.
Can I listen to music at the same time?
Yes. Rainy Desk is designed to mix with other audio apps. Start your music first, then open Rainy Desk.
Will the rain keep playing when I lock my phone?
Yes. The rain audio continues in the background even with the screen locked, as long as the app is running.
What does the sleep timer do?
The sleep timer automatically stops the rain (and lightning) after a set duration — 10 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 3 hours, or 10 hours. You'll receive a notification when the timer starts and when it ends.
Why does the app ask for camera permission?
Rainy Desk uses the camera flash (torch) to simulate lightning. No photos or videos are ever taken or stored.
