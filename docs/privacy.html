🌧 Rainy Desk

Privacy Policy  ·  Last updated: March 2025

Overview

Rainy Desk ("the app") is developed by Bee's Knees. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

In short: Rainy Desk does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Data We Do Not Collect

Rainy Desk does not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, device ID)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Photos, videos, or any camera media
  • Any data transmitted to external servers

Camera & Flash

The app requests camera permission solely to access the device's flashlight (torch) for lightning simulation. No photos or videos are taken, accessed, or stored at any time.

Local Notifications

Rainy Desk may send local notifications to inform you when the sleep timer starts or ends. These notifications are generated entirely on-device and are not transmitted anywhere.

Local Storage

App settings (volume, timer preferences, lightning frequency) are saved locally on your device using iOS UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

Rainy Desk does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics services, or advertising networks.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
